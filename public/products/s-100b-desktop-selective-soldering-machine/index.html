Operating sequence

A local solder wave for selected joints

A connector or transformer may still need through-hole soldering after reflow. Program the required locations and choose a nozzle that clears the neighboring components.

Apply flux

The jet valve deposits flux at the programmed locations on the underside of the board.

Preheat the joint area

Nitrogen preheating prepares the selected region before solder contact. The installation requires a nitrogen supply.

Follow the solder path

The XYZ system positions the solder nozzle along the programmed path. Review joint access, thermal mass and nearby components on a sample board.

Application review

Before you specify

Is it suitable for short production runs?

The desktop system is intended for high-mix, low-volume THT work. A sample PCB and joint list help establish the program and nozzle selection for each board.

Can the nozzle reach a closely spaced connector?

Send the underside layout with neighboring component heights and the connector pitch. Nozzle access and thermal demand should be reviewed together.
