Machine capability

Inside the S7020T.

Open a capability to see how it is used in the insertion cycle. Published facts are separated from configuration advice.

The camera compares actual PCB hole positions with NC data and applies correction. The manual also lists visual programming and insertion-result checking.
Published table resolution is 0.001 mm/pulse, with 0.025 mm point-to-point positioning and 0.05 mm repeat positioning.
Insertion force detection is specified as an option. Include it in the final configuration if the application requires an over-force stop condition.
The published special-functions list includes material QR code scanning to support material confirmation before production.
Automatic width adjustment is listed among the machine special functions; conveyor and line interfaces remain subject to the selected handling configuration.
Programming functions listed in the manual include online visual programming, visual correction and Excel-format data support.

Installation planning

Set Up the Services Before the Machine Arrives.

The final drawing takes priority. These published requirements are a practical planning starting point for your facilities and commissioning team.

01

Power

AC220V ±10V, single phase, 50/60 Hz. Source documents state 1.6 kVA / 2.0 kW max and 2 kVA; plan the circuit to the final electrical drawing.

02

Air

Published requirement is 5–6 kgf/cm² and around 0.6 m³/min. Regulate air at the machine rather than assuming line pressure is stable.

03

Environment

Manual states 5–25 °C and 20–95% RH; solution sheet states 30–70% RH. Use the tighter condition in your local site plan.

04

Before start-up

Confirm power, air gauge and a clear working area. Follow the current operating manual and your site safety procedure.

05

Industrial PC

The manual identifies the computer as dedicated to the machine. Keep control software and media under your qualified maintenance process.

06

Line integration

Manual loading or automatic load/unload affects conveyor interfaces, board flow and floor layout. Review them with the final configuration.
