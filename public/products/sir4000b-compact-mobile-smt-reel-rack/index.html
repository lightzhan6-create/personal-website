Application review

Planning line-side reel storage

Can I store both reel sizes at the same time?

Yes. The SIR4000B catalog configuration provides 300 positions for 7-inch reels plus 23 for 13-inch reels. Check the width of your thickest reels against the listed 18 mm and 37 mm slot widths before specifying the rack.

How does light-guided picking work?

The reel ID is associated with a storage location. An LED at that location guides the operator to the requested reel. For an ERP or MES project, share your reel barcode format and work-order process so the interface can be reviewed.

What should I measure before ordering?

Measure the largest reel diameter and thickness in your kit. Record how many of each size move together, the narrowest opening on the route and which sides operators can access when the rack is parked.
