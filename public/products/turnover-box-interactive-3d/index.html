Material & Market Terminology

ESD-safe corrugated polypropylene packaging.

In international electronics packaging, this product family is commonly described with terms such as ESD divider tray, ESD corrugated plastic box, PP corrugated divider tray and ESD grid divider box.

✓
Corrugated Polypropylene (PP)Also called PP corrugated sheet, PP hollow sheet or twin-wall polypropylene sheet.
✓
Reusable Industrial PackagingLightweight, moisture-resistant, chemical-resistant and easy to fabricate into custom cells and partitions.
✓
ESD Material SpecificationConductive, dissipative and other ESD material properties are distinct classifications. The required electrical grade is defined for each project before production.
Reference Size Options

Start from a common footprint — or build from zero.

Conductive and ESD tote systems commonly use modular 300 × 200 mm, 400 × 300 mm and 600 × 400 mm footprints. We can use these as design starting points when they fit the customer's rack, cart or logistics system, or engineer a completely custom footprint.

Compact Reference
300 × 200 mm

Useful starting footprint for compact electronic parts and small line-side kits. Tray height and cell layout are customized to the component.

Euro Modular Reference
400 × 300 mm

A widely used modular footprint for ESD totes, racks and internal logistics systems. Divider geometry remains project-specific.

Euro Modular Reference
600 × 400 mm

A common larger footprint for higher part counts, larger assemblies and pallet-modular factory handling.

Typical Applications

Organized, separated handling for electronics manufacturing.

Custom divider trays are used when electronic parts need controlled orientation, physical separation, repeatable quantity management and reusable movement between warehouse, kitting and production areas.

01

Connectors & Terminals

Keep parts separated, oriented and counted before assembly or odd-form insertion.

02

PCB Assemblies

Separate boards or subassemblies during work-in-process handling and internal transfer.

03

Sensors & Electronic Modules

Dedicated cells reduce direct part-to-part contact and simplify line-side material presentation.

04

Precision Components

Custom partitions support fragile or geometry-sensitive parts that do not fit standard containers efficiently.
