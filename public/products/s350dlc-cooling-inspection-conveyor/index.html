Trace the board path

Start at the entrance and follow the rail path under the overhead structure. Check clearance against your tallest populated board and the tooling used to carry it.

Review visibility

Choose representative solder joints and connector features as inspection targets. Observe them from the normal operating position and check for reflections from shiny metal.

Evaluate the airflow

Cooling performance depends on component mass, incoming temperature and time inside the station. Measure the part of interest on a production sample before specifying an exit temperature.

Plan the release

Agree on when the operator may hold a board and when the next machine can receive it. Test both conditions with the required line direction and working height.
