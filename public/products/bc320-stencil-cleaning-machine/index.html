Integrated Process

A Clear, Repeatable Cleaning Sequence

A five-step workflow brings aqueous washing, DI water rinsing and hot-air drying together in one BC320 cycle.

01

Load the Stencil

Place the workpiece into the cleaning chamber.

02

Aqueous Wash

Water-based cleaning liquid is sprayed onto the stencil.

03

DI Water Rinse

DI water rinses remaining cleaning residues.

04

Hot Air Dry

Hot-air drying removes residual moisture after rinsing.

05

Unload

Remove the cleaned, dried stencil after the cycle.

Working Principle

Two Media Loops, One Integrated Process

Wash liquid and DI water follow separate circulation paths, each with 1 μm filtration and recovery back to its respective tank.

WASH LOOP
  • Cleaning liquid
  • Pump
  • Spray nozzles
  • Stencil
  • 1 μm filtration
  • Liquid recovery
RINSE LOOP
  • DI water
  • Pump
  • Spray nozzles
  • Stencil
  • 1 μm filtration
  • Water recovery
↗Hot Air Drying follows the wash and DI water rinse stages.

Machine Structure

Three Main Process Modules

  1. Wash

    Water-based cleaning liquid is sprayed onto the stencil. Cleaning time and liquid temperature can be adjusted.

  2. Rinse

    DI water rinses remaining cleaning residues. Rinse time and water temperature can be configured.

  3. Dry

    Hot-air drying removes residual moisture after rinsing. Drying time and hot-air temperature can be adjusted.

FAQ

BC320 Buyer Questions

Answers below are limited to the supplied product introduction and machine specification.

What can the BC320 clean?

It is presented for SMT solder paste stencils, red glue stencils, red glue copper stencils, solder paste misprinted PCBs, squeegees and PCBA.

What is the maximum stencil size?

The maximum stencil size is 737 × 737 × 40 mm.

Does the BC320 use DI water?

Yes. DI water is specified as the rinse medium, with a 60 L DI water tank.

How is the stencil dried?

The machine specification lists hot air blow drying, with a hot-air temperature range of 0–90°C.

What filtration precision does the machine use?

Cleaning and rinse filtration are both specified as 1 μm.

What utilities are required for installation?

The machine is specified for AC 380 V, 50/60 Hz power, rated at 28 kW, and compressed air at 0.5–0.7 MPa. It uses water-based cleaning liquid and DI water.
