Application review

Specify the discharge

Send the wave exit drawing, carrier mass, line speed and desired temperature at handoff.

Meet the exit rails

Start at the wave machine exit and check how the loaded carrier reaches the receiving rails. Record the transfer height, direction of travel and the shape of the pallet underside.

Follow the cooling zone

The fan moves air while the assembly travels along the outfeed path. A dense connector and a thin board edge may cool at different rates.

Measure the load

Choose a temperature measurement point that matters to the downstream process. Run a representative pallet at production speed and record that point at discharge.

Review downstream handling

Check that the next station can receive the longest carrier and support its leading edge. Allow room for normal operator access and for the downstream handling fixture.
