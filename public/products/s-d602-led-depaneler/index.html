Designed for controlled separation

Keep the cut path stable from first contact to final separation.

When a V-cut board needs to be separated repeatedly, the cutting path and board support matter as much as the blade itself. This machine uses three pairs of blades to follow the microgroove in stages, with a platform arrangement designed for practical handling of long boards.

The supplied specification describes smooth separation with reduced risk of board, component and solder-joint defects after breakout.

Three-stage cutting path

Three pairs of blades divide the cutting action and help the machine locate the V-cut consistently.

The six-knife arrangement is specified for V-cut PCB, aluminum PCB, FR-4, LED PCB and PCBA work.

A 2400 mm platform is listed in the supplied description for long-board handling and board fall-away.

The supplied data describes front and rear blade calibration using a double-frequency laser interferometry instrument.

Working principle

Align. Cut. Release.

A simple operating sequence keeps the focus on the V-cut groove and repeatable board flow.

STEP 01

Align the board

Position the PCB so the microgroove is presented to the blade path. The supplied limit requires more than 1 mm between components and the groove.

STEP 02

Cut in three stages

Three pairs of knives engage the V-cut in sequence to support a controlled separation process across the board.

STEP 03

Release the panel

The angled platform arrangement allows the processed board to fall away from the working surface.

Questions before RFQ

What should be confirmed?

A short pre-sales checklist keeps the machine configuration aligned with your actual PCB process.

What board types can it process?

The supplied document lists V-cut PCB, aluminum PCB, FR-4, LED PCB, PCBA and glass fibre board among the applicable materials.

What is the blade arrangement?

The machine uses three pairs of blades, six knives in total, for a staged cutting path along the V-cut groove.

What board information should I send?

Send board material, thickness, length, V-cut layout, distance from components to groove, and expected output per minute.

Is the cutting length really unlimited?

Unlimited is the value in the supplied specification. Confirm the actual board and platform arrangement with the final configuration before quotation.

Can it be supplied for 110V or 220V?

The supplied data lists 110V or 220V and 50Hz or 60Hz. Final electrical configuration should be confirmed for the destination market.

Can you test my board?

Yes. Include a board sample or clear drawings with the RFQ so the cutting path and application limit can be reviewed.

Prepare the next step

Send the board details. We will match the cutting path to your application.

For a fast equipment review, include the PCB material, thickness, V-cut drawing, longest board length, component clearance and target capacity.
