Set the job at the control panel

The Chinese / English panel combines an LCD with a keypad. The machine uses an SKD-11 cross-cutting blade, a stainless steel panel and rubber feed rollers.

Add longitudinal slitting

Optional slitting blades make longitudinal cuts while the cross-cutting blade sets the sheet length. Share the required strip widths when requesting the configuration.
