Application review

Review your line

Send the board dimensions, transport direction, fixed rail side and required working height.

Start at the rail

Look along the two rails and identify the fixed side of your production line. That reference determines how the incoming board should line up as it enters the station.

Support the edges

Check the board edges against the narrowest and widest products in the job. An underside connector or an overhanging panel tab may change the available support area.

Match the span

This particular drawing is labeled as a one meter conveyor. Measure the clear space between both host machines before selecting the length for your installation.

Test the handoff

Follow a sample board from the upstream rails onto the link and across the exit gap. Use the actual panel and record any hesitation at either transition.
